MOROCCO · AEROSPACE

ABOUT THE CREATOR

SARA ELMAGHFOR

YOUNG MOROCCAN AEROSPACE ENTHUSIAST

A young Moroccan aerospace enthusiast and the creator of SAEL Aerospace.

This project was created to make aircraft systems easier to understand, explore, and remember — especially for young people who are curious about aviation and aerospace.

Through interactive visuals, simple explanations, and hands-on exploration, SAEL Aerospace turns complex aircraft concepts into something more accessible, engaging, and noticeable.

Built from curiosity. Designed for future aerospace enthusiasts.
